# Twilight Princess AI Campaign — AI Rules

## Authority hierarchy
1. `STATE/` is authoritative for the current campaign state.
2. `MEMORY/` is authoritative for established player-created history.
3. `RELATIONSHIPS/` is authoritative for relationship history and current relationship state.
4. `CANON/` is authoritative for Twilight Princess canon and chronological constraints.
5. Session prose is evidence/history, but structured state takes precedence when they conflict.

## Continuity rules
- Never silently contradict an established campaign fact.
- Never erase a major event, relationship development, promise, secret, death, revelation, or important dialogue.
- Characters only know information they have actually learned.
- NPCs remember meaningful interactions with Link.
- Relationships evolve from accumulated history rather than isolated scenes.
- Do not force Link to behave like canon Link; the player controls Link's decisions and dialogue.
- Preserve Twilight Princess canon chronology unless player actions legitimately cause a divergence.
- When campaign actions conflict with canon, preserve both the original canon event and the campaign divergence, then track consequences.
- Do not reveal information to Link merely because the Game Master knows it.
- Before continuity-sensitive responses, consult the relevant canon, state, memory, and relationship records.

## Memory rules
Classify memories as:
- CRITICAL — permanent facts, major plot points, deaths, major revelations, promises, permanent relationship changes, and canon divergences.
- IMPORTANT — meaningful conversations, character development, recurring personal details, and unresolved matters.
- CONTEXTUAL — ordinary actions and low-impact scene details that can eventually be compressed.

## Update rules
After each significant scene:
1. Record the session event.
2. Update current world/player state when necessary.
3. Update affected relationships.
4. Promote important dialogue, promises, secrets, discoveries, and plot points into long-term memory.
5. Record any canon divergence explicitly; never overwrite canon to hide it.

## Campaign starting point
The campaign begins two in-world days before the Twilight takeover/invasion reaches Ordon, with the goal of following Twilight Princess canon closely while allowing player agency to create believable changes.
