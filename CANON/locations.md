# Twilight Princess Location Reference

## Ordona Province
Rural southern region associated with Ordon Village. Link's home and ranch life are here. It is the campaign's starting region.

## Ordon Village
Link's hometown. Key locations include Link's House, Ordon Ranch, the village square, Sera's Sundries, Ordon Spring, and paths toward Ordon Woods.

## Ordon Spring
A sacred spring near the village and a recurring early-story location. It is important to Link, Ilia, Epona, and the early Twilight incident.

## Faron Province / Faron Woods
Forested region north of Ordona. The early monkey rescue occurs here, and later the region is affected by Twilight. The Forest Temple lies deeper in the woods.

## Eldin Province
Region containing Kakariko Village and Death Mountain. It becomes a major early destination after Faron.

## Kakariko Village
Settlement in Eldin Province. Its early-story condition is dramatically affected by the Twilight and monster activity.

## Death Mountain
Home of the Gorons and location of the Goron Mines.

## Lanayru Province
Large region containing Hyrule Castle Town, Lake Hylia, and Lakebed Temple-related areas.

## Sacred Grove
Ancient forested/sacred area tied to the Master Sword, later progression, and the Temple of Time.

## Gerudo Desert
Desert region containing the Arbiter's Grounds.

## Snowpeak
Cold mountainous region containing Snowpeak Ruins.

## Temple of Time
Ancient temple accessed through the Sacred Grove; important to the later story and time-travel sequence.

## City in the Sky
Late-game dungeon/region associated with the Oocca.

## Twilight Realm
Realm of the Twili and destination of the later campaign after the confrontation with Zant.

## Hyrule Castle
Final major story location and site of the concluding confrontation.
