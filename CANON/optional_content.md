# Optional and Side Content Integration

Optional content is part of the world rather than filler. It may be encountered when location, time, abilities, and story state make it plausible.

## Canon exception: Ending Blow

**Ending Blow is mandatory campaign content.** It is the first Hidden Skill taught by the Hero's Shade and is treated as part of the main canon progression immediately before Link enters the Forest Temple.

### Required sequence
- Link has reached the Sacred Grove/Faron progression and is preparing to enter the Forest Temple.
- The Hero's Shade encounter occurs at the appropriate point.
- Link is taught **Ending Blow**.
- The skill is recorded as permanently learned.
- Only after this checkpoint does the campaign proceed into the Forest Temple chapter.

### Narrative rule
Ending Blow should feel like a natural part of Link's growth as a swordsman rather than an optional collectible. The remaining Hidden Skills remain optional unless another canon dependency is explicitly established.

## Combat / Hero's Shade — remaining skills
- Hidden Skills after Ending Blow are optional training opportunities.
- Hero's Shade encounters can be remembered as meaningful training sessions.
- The GM should not force later skills if Link never encounters the teacher.
- The Hero's Shade's identity/history should not be spoiled early.

## Collectibles
Track persistent collection progress when the player chooses to pursue it:
- Heart Pieces / Heart Containers
- Golden Bugs
- Poe Souls
- Hidden Skills other than Ending Blow
- Howling Stones
- Great Fairy rewards
- Wallet upgrades
- Bomb Bag upgrades
- Quiver upgrades
- Fishing-related progression

## Agitha / Golden Bugs
Agitha's collection is an optional Castle Town relationship. Each Golden Bug returned can be remembered as a separate interaction/reward rather than a generic currency transaction.

## Jovani / Poe Souls
Jovani's curse and his requests create an optional long-term side story. If Link begins helping him, the campaign should remember how many souls have been delivered and how Jovani reacts.

## Cave of Ordeals
Can be attempted once the world state and equipment make it plausible. Its floors are optional challenge content and should not be inserted into the critical path.

## Fishing Hole
Hena and the fishing area can support relaxed character scenes, including Link/Ilia scenes when the campaign's location and timing permit.

## Malo Mart
Malo's development into an entrepreneur can be tracked as an independent character arc. The Castle Town branch should only exist after its prerequisites are met.

## Magic Armor
Optional acquisition. It should not be assumed to exist simply because Link reaches late-game areas.

## Hawkeye
Optional ranged upgrade. Track acquisition separately from the Bow.

## Bombs and Bomb Bags
Track individual upgrades/variants if acquired. Do not assume infinite bombs or late-game capacity before the relevant upgrade.

## Hearts and combat strength
The campaign may track health upgrades and major equipment, but combat difficulty should not be reduced to hidden numerical calculations. Link's actual condition matters narratively.

## Side-content insertion rule
For every optional event, verify:
- current day/time
- current location
- required item/ability
- whether the relevant NPC is available
- whether the relevant story chapter has occurred
- whether the event conflicts with an urgent canon situation

If it does not fit, the GM should offer it later rather than forcing it into the current scene.
