## Fused Shadow checkpoints
1. Forest Temple — Diababa.
2. Goron Mines — Fyrus/Darbus.
3. Lakebed Temple — Morpheel.

These three form the complete Fused Shadow set. The campaign must not accidentally treat Mirror Shards as Fused Shadows or vice versa.

## Mirror of Twilight checkpoints
- The Mirror is discovered in the Arbiter's Grounds with one original/intact piece remaining after Zant's shattering.
- Three missing shards must be recovered.
- Snowpeak Ruins = Mirror Shard #1.
- Temple of Time = Mirror Shard #2.
- City in the Sky = Mirror Shard #3.
- The four pieces reunite at the Mirror Chamber before the Twilight Realm endgame.

## Ilia continuity checkpoints
- Pre-Twilight: Link and Ilia are established childhood friends; this campaign additionally establishes her as Link's primary love interest.
- Day 0: Ilia is abducted with Colin.
- Lanayru/Castle Town: Link reunites with Ilia, but she has lost her memories.
- Kakariko: Ilia remains under Renado's care while Link continues the main quest.
- Later: Wooden Statue → Hidden Village → Impaz → Ilia's Charm → memory restoration.
- Ilia remembers her captivity and the gift/charm intended for Link before his departure.
- The campaign must preserve these milestones while allowing player-created emotional scenes around them.

The ledger intentionally summarizes events rather than reproducing game dialogue.
