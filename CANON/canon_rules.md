# Final Canon Enforcement Rules

This file is authoritative when a broad category such as "optional content" conflicts with a specific canon dependency.

## Mandatory pre-Forest Temple checkpoint: Ending Blow

The first Hero's Shade encounter and **Ending Blow** are mandatory campaign progression.

The sequence is:

1. Link completes the required early Faron/Ordon progression.
2. Link is in the Sacred Grove/Faron area and is preparing for the Forest Temple.
3. The Hero's Shade appears at the canonical encounter point.
4. Link is taught **Ending Blow**.
5. `STATE/canon_status.json` records `ending_blow = learned`.
6. Link may then proceed into the Forest Temple.

The player still controls Link's attitude, questions, emotions, and actions during the encounter. The GM does not control Link's internal reaction.

No other Hidden Skill is mandatory unless a later campaign revision explicitly adds a canon dependency.

## Canon-versus-optional hierarchy

When rules conflict, use this priority:

1. Explicit mandatory canon event.
2. Required item/progression dependency.
3. Canon character knowledge and behavior.
4. Canon world-state consequence.
5. Optional side quest.
6. Cosmetic/background detail.

A specific mandatory checkpoint overrides a generic statement that its category is optional.

## Canon progression integrity

A major canon event cannot be silently skipped because the player is free to explore. Exploration may occur around the event when the timeline permits, but required story gates remain intact.

## Player agency

The player controls Link completely:
- dialogue choices
- decisions
- emotional reactions
- romance choices
- combat decisions
- exploration
- optional quest participation
- how Link responds to canon events

The GM controls everyone and everything outside Link, subject to established character motivations, knowledge, and canon.

The GM must never write Link's consequential choice for him and then present it as if the player chose it.

## Canon divergence

If Link's action makes a canonical event impossible or substantially different:
- do not force the original result;
- record the cause;
- preserve consequences;
- determine whether later events remain possible;
- allow the campaign to diverge naturally.

The goal is strict canon **until Link actually changes it**, not railroading disguised as canon.
