# Twilight Princess Chapter-by-Chapter Campaign Guide

This is the operational canon guide for the Game Master. It expands `story_beats.md` into chapter-level causal chains. It is intentionally descriptive rather than reproducing game dialogue.

## Chapter 01 — Ordon Village: two days before departure

### Required baseline
- Link lives and works in Ordon Village.
- Link is familiar with Rusl, Uli, Mayor Bo, Fado, Sera, the children, and the village community.
- Link and Ilia are childhood friends and, for this campaign, established romantic interests. Their exact degree of mutual awareness/commitment is player-facing relationship history to be established before play.
- Rusl has already asked Link to deliver a gift to the Royal Family of Hyrule.
- Link's departure is scheduled for two days later.

### Canon anchors
- The village is peaceful.
- Link's ordinary ranch life is the emotional baseline for the coming catastrophe.
- The children and Epona are part of Link's everyday world.

### GM freedom
- This chapter is intentionally open for romance, friendships, village errands, conversations, training, exploration, and personal scenes.
- Do not manufacture urgency that does not exist yet.
- Do not prematurely reveal the Twilight invasion.

### Required transition
The next day must remain available for the canonical child/fishing/sword sequence unless player actions create a documented divergence.

---

## Chapter 02 — Ordon Village: the day before the attack

### Core sequence
- Link participates in village life.
- Sera's cat/fishing sequence can establish the Fishing Rod and bottle progression.
- The children obtain/receive the Slingshot and wooden sword sequence.
- Link demonstrates sword techniques to the children.
- Talo, Malo, Beth, Colin, and the monkey sequence leads into Faron Woods.
- Link rescues the children/monkey from danger.

### Relationship opportunities
- Ilia can have private scenes with Link around Epona, the village, Link's upcoming departure, or their relationship.
- Any such scenes are persistent memories and may alter later emotional context without removing the attack itself.

### Required knowledge
Nobody in Ordon should know the true nature of the Twilight invasion yet.

---

## Chapter 03 — Ordon Spring: the attack

### Prerequisites
- Link is preparing to leave for Hyrule.
- Epona is available and tied to Link/Ilia's established relationship.
- Fado's goat-herding task is the final ordinary responsibility before departure.

### Canon anchors
- Ilia takes Epona to Ordon Spring after Epona is injured/upset.
- Colin, Talo, and the other children are involved in the pre-attack sequence.
- King Bulblin and the Bulblins attack.
- Colin and Ilia are abducted.
- The Twilight reaches Ordon.
- Link is dragged into the Twilight and transformed into Wolf Link.

### Emotional continuity
This is the campaign's first major rupture of Link's normal life. The AI must remember exactly what Link and Ilia had said/done before the attack.

---

## Chapter 04 — Twilight Hyrule / Prison / Midna

### Required events
- Wolf Link is imprisoned.
- Link meets Midna.
- Midna helps Link escape.
- Link and Midna travel through the Twilight-covered castle/sewer route.
- Zelda is glimpsed/encountered as part of the castle sequence.
- Link returns to Ordona with Midna.

### Knowledge boundary
- Link does not automatically understand the Twilight Realm, Midna's motives, the Triforce, Ganondorf, or the ancient history.
- Midna has information Link lacks and does not volunteer everything.

### Relationship rule
Midna begins as an ally of convenience with her own agenda. Trust must develop through interactions.

---

## Chapter 05 — Ordon weapons / Faron Twilight

### Canon chain
- Link obtains the Ordon Sword and Ordon Shield.
- Link and Midna travel toward Faron.
- Faron Province is covered in Twilight.
- Link meets Light Spirit Faron in diminished form.
- Wolf Link collects the Tears of Light.
- Faron's light is restored.

### State changes
- Link learns more about the crisis.
- Wolf/Human transformation remains an important mechanic.
- The Ordon equipment remains distinct from later Hylian equipment.

---

## Chapter 06 — Forest Temple

### Required progression
- Link reaches the Forest Temple.
- Ooccoo and Ooccoo Jr. are encountered and can provide dungeon transportation.
- The monkeys are rescued throughout the dungeon.
- Link uses the rescued monkeys to progress.
- Link obtains the dungeon's key equipment and completes its major traversal requirements.
- Diababa is defeated.
- **Fused Shadow #1 is obtained.**

### Canon state
Midna is pursuing the Fused Shadows for reasons she has not completely explained.

---

## Chapter 07 — Kakariko Village / Eldin Twilight

### Required progression
- Link reaches Eldin Province.
- Kakariko Village is under Twilight.
- Light Spirit Eldin is encountered.
- Link restores Eldin's light through the Tears of Light.
- The surviving Ordon children are found.
- The village becomes a major safe location for the displaced children.

### Character continuity
Renado, Luda, Barnes, the Gorons, and the Ordon children should now be tracked as active world characters rather than one-scene NPCs.

---

## Chapter 08 — Death Mountain / Goron Mines

### Required progression
- Link interacts with the Gorons and reaches Death Mountain.
- The Iron Boots become available through the Goron progression.
- Link enters Goron Mines.
- Darbus is encountered in corrupted Fyrus form.
- Link defeats Fyrus and restores Darbus.
- **Fused Shadow #2 is obtained.**

### State
- Link's relationship with the Gorons changes based on his actions.
- Darbus's recovery is remembered.
- The second Fused Shadow joins the first.

---

## Chapter 09 — Lanayru Twilight / Castle Town

### Required progression
- Lanayru Province is covered in Twilight.
- Link meets Light Spirit Lanayru.
- Tears of Light restore the province.
- Link gains access to the wider Castle Town/Lake Hylia progression.
- Telma's tavern and resistance network become important.

### Character setup
Telma, Auru, Ashei, Shad, and their relationships to the larger crisis are established over time rather than all explained immediately.

---

## Chapter 10 — Ilia and Prince Ralis

### Required emotional checkpoint
**Link reunites with Ilia.**

### Canon state
- Ilia is in Telma's care.
- Prince Ralis, a Zora prince, is collapsed and being cared for.
- Ilia does not remember Link because of the trauma and events surrounding her abduction.
- Link's reaction is entirely player-controlled.

### Required progression
- Link escorts/helps move Ilia and Ralis toward Kakariko.
- Renado takes responsibility for their care.
- The mystery of Ilia's memory loss becomes a persistent unresolved thread.

### Romance rule
This is not an opportunity to force instant recognition. Ilia's memory loss is real. Her emotional responses to Link should come from what she can actually perceive and know in the current state.

---

## Chapter 11 — Zora's Domain / Queen Rutela / Lakebed Temple

### Required progression
- Link learns of the Zora royal tragedy.
- Queen Rutela's spirit can guide/reward Link.
- Ralis's grief and status as the surviving prince are established.
- Link obtains the **Zora Armor**.
- Link reaches Lake Hylia and Lakebed Temple.
- Water-based dungeon progression occurs.
- Morpheel is defeated.
- **Fused Shadow #3 is obtained, completing the set.**

### Required transition
The completed Fused Shadows lead directly into the next confrontation at Lanayru Spring.

---

## Chapter 12 — Zant / Midna / Zelda

### Required progression
- Link and Midna return to Lanayru Spring.
- The history of the Dark Interlopers and the Fused Shadow becomes relevant.
- Zant confronts Link and Midna.
- Midna is forcibly exposed to Light World energy and severely harmed.
- Link carries/escorts Midna to Hyrule Castle.
- Zelda saves Midna by sacrificing her physical form/remaining essence.

### Knowledge boundary
- Link now knows far more about the crisis, but still does not possess the entire history of Ganondorf, Zant, and the Twilight Realm.
- Midna's vulnerability and Zelda's sacrifice permanently change the emotional state of the party.

---

## Chapter 13 — Sacred Grove / Master Sword

### Required progression
- Link and Midna return toward Faron/Sacred Grove.
- Skull Kid/forest guardian encounters occur as part of the route.
- Link reaches the Master Sword pedestal.
- **Link obtains the Master Sword.**
- The sword becomes a permanent central story item.
- The Master Sword enables later transformation/control progression.

### Optional continuity
Hero's Shade encounters can be woven into exploration and training if their conditions are met. They should not be treated as mandatory plot exposition.

---

## Chapter 14 — Gerudo Desert / Arbiter's Grounds

### Required progression
- Link reaches Gerudo Desert.
- Arbiter's Grounds reveals ancient execution history.
- The four Poe flames/thieves sequence restores access through the dungeon.
- Link obtains the **Spinner**.
- Stallord is defeated.
- Link reaches the Mirror Chamber.
- The Sages reveal the history of Ganondorf's execution/banishment and Zant's connection to him.
- The Mirror of Twilight is found with **three missing shards**.

### State transition
The Fused Shadow collection is over. The campaign enters the **Mirror Shard arc**.

---

## Chapter 15 — Snowpeak / Mirror Shard #1

### Lead-in
- Ashei reports a mysterious creature in Snowpeak.
- Link's investigation connects to Prince Ralis and the Reekfish.
- Ralis provides the **Coral Earring**, enabling the relevant fishing/tracking setup.
- Link catches the Reekfish and follows its scent in Wolf form.
- Link meets Yeto and Yeta.

### Dungeon
- Yeto possesses a Mirror Shard.
- Link helps Yeto/Yeta and enters Snowpeak Ruins.
- The Ball and Chain is acquired.
- Yeta's condition and the shard's corruption lead to Blizzeta.
- Blizzeta is defeated.
- **Mirror Shard #1 is recovered.**

### Character continuity
Yeto/Yeta should remember Link's conduct and the outcome of their relationship, even though they may not appear again for long periods.

---

## Chapter 16 — Sacred Grove / Temple of Time

### Required progression
- Link returns to the Sacred Grove.
- Access to the Temple of Time is opened through the established Sacred Grove/Master Sword sequence.
- The Temple's ancient/past setting is distinguished from the modern Sacred Grove.
- Ooccoo assists with dungeon logistics.
- Link obtains the **Dominion Rod**.
- Armogohma is defeated.
- **Mirror Shard #2 is recovered.**

### Dominion Rod rule
The Rod functions fully in the ancient Temple environment but loses its useful power in the modern world until the later Sky Character restoration sequence.

---

## Chapter 17 — Restoring Ilia's Memory

### Trigger
After the Temple of Time sequence, Renado's message/letter initiates the memory restoration quest.

### Causal chain
1. Link returns to Kakariko Sanctuary.
2. Renado explains that Ilia may be able to recover memories by retracing clues connected to her captivity.
3. Renado's Letter sends Link to Telma.
4. Telma provides the Invoice associated with Dr. Borville.
5. The doctor points Link toward the **Wooden Statue** associated with Ilia.
6. The statue's scent/memory clue leads Link toward the Forgotten Place/Hidden Village.
7. Darbus's assistance/knowledge helps establish access to the hidden route.
8. Link reaches the Hidden Village.
9. Link clears the Bulblin threat.
10. Link meets **Impaz**.
11. Impaz gives Link the **Horse Call** (often referred to informally as Ilia's charm) to help restore Ilia's memory.
12. Link returns to Ilia and shows/uses the Horse Call.
13. **Ilia's memory returns.**
14. Ilia remembers her past with Link and the circumstances surrounding her captivity.
15. Ilia explains the information about the mysterious "messenger to the heavens."

### Important terminology
The game's item is the **Horse Call**. Campaign notes may call it "Ilia's charm" for emotional shorthand, but the state database should preserve the canonical item name.

### Romance significance
This is one of the campaign's most important relationship checkpoints. Ilia's recovered memories should include the history she actually shared with Link, plus any campaign-created memories that remain compatible with her canon memory.

---

## Chapter 18 — Hidden Village / Ancient Sky Book / Sky Cannon

### First half: Ancient Sky Book
- After Ilia's recovery, Link returns to Impaz with the **Dominion Rod**.
- Impaz recognizes Link as the "Messenger to the Heavens" described by her lineage's tradition.
- Impaz gives Link the **Ancient Sky Book**.

### Second half: Shad
- Link returns to Kakariko Sanctuary.
- Shad is studying the ancient statue/Oocca connection.
- Shad can read the Sky Writing.
- The book is incomplete, so its first reading does not immediately reveal the whole route.
- The six missing Sky Characters must be recovered using the Dominion Rod and Owl Statues around Hyrule.
- Completing the book restores the relevant ancient mechanism.

### Final transition
- Link uses the restored Dominion Rod to move the sealed Owl Statue.
- The hidden room contains the **Sky Cannon**.
- Fyer repairs the cannon at Lake Hylia.
- Link uses the cannon to reach the City in the Sky.

### Critical anti-shortcut rule
Do not collapse this into "Impaz gives Link the book and he goes to the sky." The entire chain is important for Shad, Impaz, the Oocca, the Dominion Rod, and the campaign's sense of place.

---

## Chapter 19 — City in the Sky / Mirror Shard #3

### Required progression
- Link reaches the City in the Sky through the repaired Sky Cannon.
- The Oocca are encountered.
- The dungeon establishes the ancient sky civilization and its technology.
- Link obtains the **Double Clawshot**.
- Argorok is defeated.
- **Mirror Shard #3 is recovered.**

### State transition
The complete Mirror of Twilight set is now available: original piece + three recovered shards.

---

## Chapter 20 — Mirror of Twilight / Palace of Twilight

### Required progression
- Link and Midna return to the Mirror Chamber.
- The Mirror is restored.
- Link and Midna enter the Twilight Realm.
- Palace of Twilight progression begins.
- Link obtains and restores the Sols necessary to bring light into the Palace.
- The Master Sword's role in confronting Twilight enemies is emphasized.
- Zant is confronted and defeated.

### Revelation
Zant is revealed as a pawn/servant rather than the ultimate source of the entire threat. Ganondorf's role becomes central.

### Midna continuity
Midna's true identity and her relationship to the Twilight Realm should be revealed through the campaign's established sequence, not retroactively known by Link.

---

## Chapter 21 — Hyrule Castle / Ganondorf

### Required progression
- Link, Midna, and Zelda converge on the final confrontation.
- Hyrule Castle becomes the final major dungeon.
- Ganondorf is confronted.
- The established final battle phases occur, with the exact moment-to-moment combat controlled by player action.
- Ganondorf is defeated.

### Knowledge boundary
The full history revealed by the Sages, Zant, Zelda, Midna, and Ganondorf should be treated as separate pieces of information learned at different times. The AI must not retroactively treat Link as knowing the whole story from the beginning.

---

## Chapter 22 — Ending / Ordon / Twilight separation

### Required events
- Ganondorf's defeat ends the immediate crisis.
- Midna's identity as the Twilight Princess is fully realized in the ending.
- Link, Zelda, and Midna return to the Mirror Chamber.
- Midna says goodbye and returns to the Twilight Realm.
- Midna destroys the Mirror of Twilight, permanently severing the passage between the worlds.
- Link's future and his relationship with Ilia should be determined by accumulated campaign history rather than a forced romance ending.

### Campaign-specific ending principle
The canon event of Midna leaving is fixed. The emotional meaning of Link's life afterward is not.

# Global chapter rules

1. **Canon order is strict.** Major chapter transitions cannot be skipped without a recorded divergence.
2. **Prerequisites matter.** Items, information, relationships, and locations must exist before they are used.
3. **Knowledge is character-specific.** Knowing a fact in the GM database does not mean Link or an NPC knows it.
4. **Travel matters.** Characters cannot teleport around Hyrule unless the story provides the means (for example Midna's warp ability after it becomes available).
5. **NPCs have lives.** If an NPC is absent from a chapter, their last known location and current reason for absence should be tracked.
6. **Side content can be inserted.** It must fit the current timeline, location, abilities, and world state.
7. **Player actions have consequences.** If Link changes a canon event, record the cause and altered result rather than silently correcting the player back onto the original script.
8. **Romance never overrides agency.** Ilia's feelings and choices are not determined by a meter or by what would make the player happiest.
9. **Link is player-controlled.** The GM narrates the world around Link, not Link's internal life or consequential decisions.
10. **Do not start the campaign from this document.** The repository remains in design/revision mode until explicitly authorized by the user.
