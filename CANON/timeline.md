# Twilight Princess Canon Timeline — Campaign Reference

## Timeline position
Twilight Princess occurs in the Child Timeline following the Child Ending of Ocarina of Time. This campaign treats the game's established chronology as the baseline canon.

## Campaign opening
**Starting point:** Two in-world days before the Twilight takeover reaches Ordon.

At the beginning of the game, Link is a young adult ranch worker in Ordon Village. Rusl asks Link to deliver a gift to the Royal Family of Hyrule and tells him the departure will occur in two days. The following day contains the village tutorial/events involving the children, fishing, the slingshot, the wooden sword, and Talo's pursuit of the monkey. The next morning, after Link's ranch work, Ilia takes Epona to Ordon Spring, and the Twilight-related inciting sequence begins later that day.

## Ordered main-story sequence
1. Ordon Village introduction and Rusl's request.
2. Day following the request: children, fishing rod, Sera's cat/bottle, slingshot, wooden sword, combat practice, monkey chase, rescue of Talo and the monkey.
3. Following morning: goat herding; Ilia takes Epona to Ordon Spring; Colin asks Link for help; Talo has Link's wooden sword; Link reaches Ordon Spring.
4. Twilight invasion reaches Ordon; Link is pulled into Twilight and transformed into a wolf; he is imprisoned and encounters Midna.
5. Link escapes with Midna; returns to Ordona and obtains the Ordon Sword and Ordon Shield.
6. Link and Midna enter Twilight-covered Faron Woods; meet the diminished Light Spirit Faron and restore the Faron Province's light by collecting Tears of Light.
7. Link regains Hylian form and learns of his role as the chosen hero; receives the Hero's Clothes and is directed toward the forest temple.
8. Forest Temple: Link and Midna recover the first Fused Shadow and defeat Diababa.
9. Faron directs them toward the Eldin Province.
10. Eldin Province/Kakariko Village in Twilight: restore the Eldin Light Spirit with Tears of Light; Link reunites with the Ordon children, but Ilia remains missing.
11. Death Mountain/Goron Mines: Link learns about the Gorons, receives the Iron Boots through Mayor Bo, defeats Gor Coron in sumo, enters the Goron Mines, defeats Fyrus/Darbus, and obtains the second Fused Shadow.
12. Lanayru Province in Twilight: restore Lanayru's light; pursue the story toward Hyrule Castle Town and Lake Hylia.
13. Lakebed Temple: defeat the dungeon boss and obtain the third Fused Shadow.
14. Sacred Grove/Master Sword: Link is guided toward the Sacred Grove and obtains the Master Sword; later story developments permit further progression toward the Arbiter's Grounds.
15. Gerudo Desert/Arbiter's Grounds: uncover the history surrounding the Twilit Mirror and confront the dungeon's threats.
16. Snowpeak: meet Yeto/Yeta and explore Snowpeak Ruins; obtain another shard of the Mirror of Twilight.
17. Sacred Grove/Temple of Time: return to the Sacred Grove, gain access to the Temple of Time, and progress through the dungeon and associated time-travel sequence.
18. City in the Sky: pursue the final major dungeon and obtain the final required progression item.
19. Palace of Twilight: enter the Twilight Realm and confront Zant.
20. Hyrule Castle: final confrontation with Ganondorf and resolution of the central conflict.

## Canon chronology rule
The numbered sequence is the high-level spine. Optional exploration, sidequests, conversations, travel, and player-created scenes may occur between appropriate main-story beats. Player actions can create divergences, but the original canon event should remain documented as the baseline rather than being silently rewritten.

## Opening-day precision
The campaign should treat the opening as a lived-in period rather than immediately rushing to the game's tutorial. The player may use the two-day pre-Twilight window for ordinary life, relationships, exploration, work, conversations, and personal decisions. Canon pressure should increase as the scheduled departure and Twilight incident approach.
