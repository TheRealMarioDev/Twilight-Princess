# Canon Knowledge Gates

The Game Master may know the entire Twilight Princess plot. Characters do not.

This file prevents spoilers and retroactive knowledge leakage.

## Link at campaign start

Link can reasonably know:
- Ordon Village and its residents.
- His own skills, work, equipment, and immediate surroundings.
- Rusl's request that he deliver a gift to the Royal Family in Hyrule.
- His personal history with Ilia and the village children.
- Ordinary knowledge of Hyrule as appropriate for a young Ordon resident.

Link should NOT automatically know:
- The Twilight Realm's true nature.
- Midna's identity or motives.
- Zant's identity.
- Ganondorf's current status or plan.
- The Dark Interlopers' history.
- The Triforce circumstances surrounding Ganondorf.
- The Master Sword's future role.
- The Mirror of Twilight.
- The Fused Shadows as a complete set.
- The Oocca's full history.

## Midna knowledge progression

### First meeting
Midna knows substantially more than Link but conceals much of it. She is motivated by her own goals and initially treats Link as a means to an end.

### Fused Shadow arc
Midna knows the Fused Shadows are important and has personal reasons for collecting them. She should not explain everything before the story's established revelations.

### Zant confrontation
Zant's attack reveals the danger posed to Midna and changes her relationship with Link.

### Zelda sacrifice
Midna learns/experiences the significance of Zelda's intervention. Link's understanding grows from the event itself.

### Mirror arc
Midna knows enough about the Twilight Realm to guide Link but still has personal secrets.

### Twilight Realm
Midna's identity and history become increasingly explicit.

## Ilia knowledge progression

### Before abduction
Ilia knows Link personally and has the campaign-established romantic history with him.

### After abduction / memory loss
Ilia does not remember Link as she did before. Do not allow old romance memories to magically persist simply because the player expects them to. Emotional familiarity may be influenced by immediate interactions, but explicit recovered memories wait for the canonical restoration sequence unless a divergence is recorded.

### Wooden Statue clue
Ilia begins recovering fragments related to her captivity and the woman who saved her.

### Horse Call
The Horse Call acts as the major memory-restoration trigger. After it is shown/used appropriately, Ilia's memory returns.

### After restoration
Ilia can remember her prior relationship with Link plus any campaign-created memories that fit the canon sequence.

## Prince Ralis knowledge
Ralis knows his identity and the tragedy involving Queen Rutela. He does not automatically know the entire Twilight crisis or Ganondorf's full history.

## Renado knowledge
Renado understands Kakariko's spiritual situation and has information about Ilia's condition. He can direct Link toward the memory-restoration chain without possessing every answer.

## Telma's network
Telma, Auru, Ashei, and Shad know different pieces of the resistance effort. They should not function as a collective hive mind.

## Impaz knowledge
Impaz knows the Hidden Village's ancient duty and the legend of the Messenger to the Heavens. She recognizes the Dominion Rod and eventually identifies Link as the messenger.

## Shad knowledge
Shad is researching the Oocca and Sky Writing. He can interpret the Ancient Sky Book but needs the missing Sky Characters to complete the message.

## Sages' knowledge
The Sages can reveal the historical context of Ganondorf, his execution, and his banishment to the Twilight Realm. Their information becomes available only when Link reaches the appropriate story sequence.

## Zelda knowledge
Zelda knows substantially more about Hyrule's crisis than Link at the beginning. She should reveal information according to her actual circumstances and the canonical sequence.

## Zant knowledge
Zant knows his own role, his relationship to Midna, and his connection to Ganondorf. He should not expose every truth merely because the GM knows it.

## Ganondorf knowledge
Ganondorf's internal plans and knowledge are never automatically available to Link, Midna, Zelda, or NPCs. They must be learned through actual revelations, observation, or inference.

## Information leakage rule
When an NPC speaks, ask internally:
1. Does this character know the fact?
2. How did they learn it?
3. Would they willingly reveal it?
4. Is the timing appropriate?

If the answer to any of these is no, the information should remain hidden.
