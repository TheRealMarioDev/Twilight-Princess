# Twilight Princess Items, Abilities, and Progression Dependencies

This file tracks meaningful equipment and the story reasons they matter. It is not a 100% collectible inventory list.

## Opening / Ordon
- Fishing Rod — early village activity; associated with the cat/fishing sequence.
- Bottle — acquired through the early Ordon/Sera sequence and useful for carrying liquids, fairies, etc.
- Slingshot — early ranged tool and child-facing tutorial item.
- Wooden Sword — given for the children's sword demonstration; later becomes relevant to the opening crisis because Talo has it.
- Ordon Sword — obtained after Link returns from the first Twilight sequence; his early real sword.
- Ordon Shield — obtained during the same early post-Twilight preparation; distinct from the Hylian Shield.

## Early Twilight / Faron / Forest Temple
- Lantern — useful for dark areas and ordinary exploration; should be tracked if acquired.
- Tears of Light — not permanent equipment, but a critical quest resource for restoring Light Spirits.
- Faron's light restoration — required to progress the early story.
- Forest Temple small keys/map/compass/big key — dungeon progression items.
- **Fused Shadow #1** — reward after Diababa.

## Eldin / Death Mountain / Goron Mines
- Iron Boots — required for underwater movement, magnetic Goron Mine traversal, and later key sequences.
- Bombs/Bomb Bag — critical for multiple progression puzzles and combat; track upgrades separately.
- Bow and Arrows — major ranged weapon obtained during the early dungeon progression and required for many later puzzles/bosses.
- **Fused Shadow #2** — reward after defeating Fyrus/Darbus.

## Lanayru / Zora / Lakebed Temple
- Hylian Shield — later defensive upgrade; distinct from Ordon Shield.
- Zora-related progression and underwater access.
- Zora Armor — granted by Queen Rutela; greatly improves swimming/underwater traversal and is tied to the Ralis/Rutela storyline.
- **Fused Shadow #3 / final Fused Shadow** — reward after Morpheel.
- Light Spirit restoration in Lanayru — required for progression.

## Master Sword / Sacred Grove
- Master Sword — obtained in the Sacred Grove after the required forest/temple progression.
- Master Sword is a permanent major story item and should never be treated as merely another sword in inventory.
- Master Sword allows Link to progress into the later story and supports the human/wolf transformation mechanics.
- Hidden Skills / Hero's Shade techniques — separate combat progression and should not be confused with mandatory story items.

## Gerudo Desert / Arbiter's Grounds
- Spinner — obtained in Arbiter's Grounds and required for the dungeon's traversal and later world traversal.
- Mirror of Twilight — discovered in the Mirror Chamber with one original/intact piece remaining after Zant's shattering.
- The Sages identify three missing shards and provide the clues leading to their locations.
- **Mirror Shard #1** — Snowpeak Ruins.
- **Mirror Shard #2** — Temple of Time.
- **Mirror Shard #3** — City in the Sky.

## Snowpeak
- Reekfish scent — temporary tracking clue rather than permanent equipment.
- Coral Earring — given by Prince Ralis and used to make the fishing setup for Reekfish effective.
- Ball and Chain — Snowpeak Ruins dungeon item; required for breaking ice and defeating/handling certain obstacles.
- **Mirror Shard #1** — obtained after Snowpeak Ruins/Blizzeta progression.

## Temple of Time
- Dominion Rod — dungeon item used to control ancient statues and solve major puzzles.
- **Mirror Shard #2** — obtained after Armogohma.
- Dominion Rod power becomes depleted after leaving the Temple of Time and is restored much later.

## Ilia memory / Hidden Village / Sky progression
- Wooden Statue — memory clue used in the Ilia restoration sequence.
- Ilia's Charm — obtained from Impaz; shown to Ilia and triggers the recovery of her memories; Ilia then gives the charm to Link.
- Ancient Sky Book — given by Impaz after Link demonstrates that he is the messenger associated with the Rod of the Heavens.
- Owl Statue Sky Writing characters — collected to unlock the sealed mechanism and restore the Dominion Rod.
- Restored Dominion Rod — required to access the Sky Cannon route.
- Sky Cannon — repaired by Fyer at Lake Hylia; launches Link to the City in the Sky.

## City in the Sky
- Double Clawshot — major traversal upgrade, allowing Link to chain clawshot targets and reach areas unavailable with the original Clawshot.
- **Mirror Shard #3** — obtained after defeating Argorok.

## Palace of Twilight / Endgame
- Sols — critical Twilight Realm light objects used to progress and restore light in the Palace.
- Light Arrows / late-game combat resources — track according to the campaign's exact progression and acquisition.
- Mirror of Twilight — fully restored once all three missing shards return to the original piece.

## Optional/upgrade equipment that can affect continuity
- Hawkeye — ranged aiming upgrade.
- Bomb Bag upgrades / Bomb variants.
- Wallet upgrades.
- Magic Armor.
- Fishing gear and related upgrades.
- Lantern and bottles.
- Ordon Shield / Hylian Shield distinctions.
- Heart Containers and Pieces of Heart.
- Golden Bug collection and rewards.
- Poe Soul collection and Jovani's reward chain.

## Progression integrity rule
The Game Master should not grant an item early merely because Link would benefit from it. Major equipment is gated by the canon event, dungeon, character, or quest that provides it unless the campaign has explicitly created and recorded a plausible divergence.

When an item is acquired, the campaign should record:
- acquisition event
- location
- source/owner
- reason it was given or found
- abilities unlocked
- characters who witnessed or know about it
- whether it is mandatory, optional, or upgrade equipment
