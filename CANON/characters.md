# Twilight Princess Canon Character Reference

This file stores concise behavioral and knowledge anchors. It is intentionally not a script transcript.

## Link
- Young adult ranch worker from Ordon Village.
- Skilled, capable, and familiar with rural life, animals, riding, and basic combat.
- Begins the campaign before knowing the true nature of the coming Twilight crisis.
- Player controls Link's decisions, spoken words, priorities, and personality development during this campaign.
- Canon knowledge must not be granted to Link automatically.

## Ilia
- Link's childhood friend and close companion.
- Daughter of Mayor Bo.
- Has a strong connection to Epona and cares deeply about Link and the people of Ordon.
- Her relationship with Link should reflect their shared history and all player-created interactions.

## Colin
- Young boy from Ordon Village.
- Looks up to Link and is connected to the other village children.
- Begins the story timid and vulnerable compared with Link.

## Talo
- One of the Ordon children.
- Energetic, impulsive, and adventurous.
- The monkey chase and rescue are key early events.

## Malo
- One of the Ordon children.
- Young but outspoken and observant.

## Beth
- One of the Ordon children.
- Part of the children's group and participates in the early village events.

## Rusl
- Link's mentor and a trusted adult in Ordon.
- Asks Link to deliver a gift to the Royal Family of Hyrule.
- His request establishes the initial departure plan.

## Fado
- Ordon ranch worker associated with the goat herd.
- Gives Link ranch work and is part of the village's everyday life.

## Mayor Bo
- Mayor of Ordon Village.
- Ilia's father.
- Strong and experienced in sumo wrestling.

## Sera
- Runs Sera's Sundries in Ordon Village.
- Her cat and the fishing sequence are part of Link's early village life.

## Midna
- Not yet met at campaign start.
- Eventually becomes Link's companion after his transformation and imprisonment in the Twilight.
- Her knowledge and motives must remain distinct from Link's knowledge.

## Zelda
- Princess of Hyrule.
- Link has not necessarily met her at campaign start; do not assume personal familiarity beyond what the campaign establishes.

## Ganondorf
- Central antagonist connected to the Child Timeline's history and the later events of Twilight Princess.
- His knowledge and activities must not be exposed to Link or other characters without an in-world basis.
