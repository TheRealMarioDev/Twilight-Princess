# Twilight Princess Canon Character Reference

This file stores behavioral, relationship, and knowledge anchors. It is intentionally not a script transcript.

## Link
- Young adult ranch worker from Ordon Village.
- Skilled, capable, and familiar with rural life, animals, riding, and basic combat.
- Begins the campaign before knowing the true nature of the coming Twilight crisis.
- **The player has complete control over Link.** The Game Master must not choose Link's dialogue, internal thoughts, emotions, decisions, relationships, priorities, or consequential actions unless the player explicitly delegates that control.
- The Game Master may describe external consequences of Link's actions and may handle trivial physical continuity when an action is unambiguously implied.
- Link's personality should emerge from player choices rather than being forced toward a fixed interpretation of canon Link.
- Canon knowledge must not be granted to Link automatically.

## Ilia
- Link's childhood friend, close companion, and **primary love interest in this campaign**.
- Daughter of Mayor Bo.
- Has a strong connection to Epona and cares deeply about Link and the people of Ordon.
- The campaign should treat Link and Ilia as having a believable pre-existing emotional history appropriate to childhood friends who have grown close.
- Romance is a major campaign thread and a major motivation available to Link, while never overriding player control.
- The relationship should have room for affection, humor, awkwardness, conflict, jealousy, fear, tenderness, separation, reconciliation, grief, hope, and lasting happiness when earned.
- Do not force a romance meter or predetermined outcome. Relationship state should emerge from choices and accumulated history.
- Ilia is an autonomous character. She may disagree with Link, make choices without him, become hurt, become frightened, express affection, set boundaries, forgive, refuse, misunderstand, or change as circumstances demand.
- Ilia must remember meaningful interactions with Link, including promises, confessions, arguments, private jokes, gifts, betrayals, rescues, and moments of vulnerability.
- The game's canon events involving Ilia remain important. Player-created romance should enrich those events rather than erase their dramatic weight.

## Colin
- Young boy from Ordon Village.
- Looks up to Link and is connected to the other village children.
- Begins the story timid and vulnerable compared with Link.
- His relationship with Link should be affected by how Link treats him before and after the Twilight crisis.

## Talo
- One of the Ordon children.
- Energetic, impulsive, and adventurous.
- The monkey chase and rescue are key early events.
- Remembers Link's treatment of him and the consequences of Link's guidance.

## Malo
- One of the Ordon children.
- Young but outspoken and observant.
- Can notice relationship dynamics and changes in village life, but should only know what he could plausibly observe or learn.

## Beth
- One of the Ordon children.
- Part of the children's group and participates in the early village events.

## Rusl
- Link's mentor and a trusted adult in Ordon.
- Asks Link to deliver a gift to the Royal Family of Hyrule.
- His request establishes the initial departure plan.
- His relationship with Link should reflect their pre-existing trust and any later player-created changes.

## Fado
- Ordon ranch worker associated with the goat herd.
- Gives Link ranch work and is part of the village's everyday life.

## Mayor Bo
- Mayor of Ordon Village.
- Ilia's father.
- Strong and experienced in sumo wrestling.
- His relationship with Link should account for Link's closeness to Ilia and Link's standing in the village.

## Sera
- Runs Sera's Sundries in Ordon Village.
- Her cat and the fishing sequence are part of Link's early village life.

## Midna
- Not yet met at campaign start.
- Eventually becomes Link's companion after his transformation and imprisonment in the Twilight.
- Her knowledge, motives, personality, and relationship with Link must develop through actual encounters rather than being prematurely exposed.
- Midna remains autonomous and can conceal, manipulate, tease, assist, distrust, or cooperate with Link according to her established motivations and campaign history.

## Zelda
- Princess of Hyrule.
- Link has not necessarily met her at campaign start; do not assume personal familiarity beyond what the campaign establishes.
- Her knowledge and motives must remain separate from Link's until revealed in-world.

## Ganondorf
- Central antagonist connected to the Child Timeline's history and the later events of Twilight Princess.
- His knowledge, plans, and activities must not be exposed to Link or other characters without an in-world basis.

## Character autonomy rule
All recurring NPCs retain agency. They remember the campaign, maintain their own goals and knowledge, and can act independently when appropriate. They are not puppets whose behavior exists only to facilitate Link's preferred outcome.
