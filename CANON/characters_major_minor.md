# Twilight Princess Major and Minor Character Reference

Characters are autonomous and have their own knowledge, goals, schedules, and memories. This file gives the Game Master anchors so that important characters are not forgotten as the campaign moves across Hyrule.

## Ordon / early campaign

### Fado
Ordon ranch worker. Gives Link goat-herding work and represents ordinary ranch life.

### Rusl
Link's mentor and trusted adult. Married to Uli and father of Colin. His request to Link starts the canonical journey toward Hyrule.

### Uli
Colin's mother and Rusl's wife. A caring Ordon villager whose concern for Colin becomes especially important after the abduction.

### Mayor Bo
Mayor of Ordon, Ilia's father, and an accomplished sumo wrestler. His relationship with Link should reflect both village familiarity and Link's closeness to Ilia.

### Sera
Owner of Sera's Sundries. Her cat is part of the opening fishing/bottle sequence.

### Coro
Forest-area shopkeeper associated with lanterns and early exploration. Track his shop and inventory when Link visits.

### Jaggle
Ordon villager and father of Malo/Beth. A minor but recurring part of village life.

### Hanch
Ordon villager and father of Talo. Track as part of Talo's family context.

### Pergie
Ordon villager associated with local village life.

### Ilia
Link's childhood friend and campaign-primary love interest. See `CANON/characters.md` for the romance-specific framework.

### Colin
Uli and Rusl's son; timid child who looks up to Link.

### Talo
Impulsive child; involved in the monkey rescue and later abduction.

### Malo
Outspoken child; later develops a strong independent personality and business ambition.

### Beth
One of the Ordon children; part of the early group and later events.

## Faron / forest characters

### Light Spirit Faron
Ancient guardian of Faron Province. Initially diminished and unable to protect the province until Link restores the Tears of Light.

### Ooccoo
A mysterious traveler who can teleport Link between dungeons and the outside world. Her presence is important to dungeon traversal.

### Ooccoo Jr.
Ooccoo's child/companion who assists with the dungeon return mechanism.

### Skull Kid
Forest-dwelling trickster/guardian figure associated with the Sacred Grove and the path to the Master Sword. The campaign should distinguish his early forest encounters from later Sacred Grove events.

## Eldin / Kakariko / Goron characters

### Renado
Shaman/leader in Kakariko Village. Provides shelter and guidance to the Ordon children and later becomes a major ally in Ilia's memory restoration.

### Luda
Renado's daughter and a Kakariko Village resident.

### Barnes
Kakariko shopkeeper/explosives specialist. His bombs and later upgrades are important to equipment progression.

### Cor Goron / Gor Coron
Goron elder/leader involved in Link's interactions with the Gorons and the aftermath of the Goron Mines.

### Darbus / Fyrus
Goron champion who becomes corrupted as Fyrus. Link defeats Fyrus in the Goron Mines, restoring Darbus. His condition is tied to the second Fused Shadow.

### Gorons
The Goron community of Death Mountain. Their distrust and later acceptance of Link should be remembered as a group relationship rather than reduced to generic NPCs.

### Malo
Later becomes associated with Malo Mart and demonstrates a surprisingly forceful entrepreneurial personality.

## Lanayru / Castle Town / resistance network

### Telma
Castle Town tavern owner and a key organizer/ally in the resistance network. She is central to Link's reunion with Ilia and Prince Ralis and introduces him to allies who provide leads for later progression.

### Louise
Telma's cat. Minor but recognizable companion at the bar.

### Auru
Telma's associate and an experienced adventurer/spy-like ally. Helps direct Link toward the Gerudo Desert/Snowpeak progression.

### Ashei
Telma's associate. Reports the mysterious Snowpeak creature and provides the sketch that begins the Reekfish/Yeto trail.

### Shad
Scholar/investigator associated with the resistance group and the Hidden Village/Sky Cannon sequence. His research into the ancient sky civilization leads directly into the Ancient Sky Book and Owl Statue progression.

### Jovani
A Castle Town man cursed into a golden form. His Poe Soul collection quest provides an optional persistent side story and reward chain.

### Agitha
Young eccentric collector in Castle Town. Runs the Golden Bug collection and rewards Link for returning Golden Bugs.

### Hena
Fishing Hole proprietor. Optional recurring character for fishing-related side content.

### Fyer
Lake Hylia's cannon operator. Repairs the Sky Cannon after the Dominion Rod/Ancient Sky Book sequence.

### Plumm
Lake Hylia-area attraction operator associated with the flight-by-chicken mini-game.

## Zora / Lanayru characters

### Queen Rutela
Deceased Zora queen whose spirit can appear to Link. Mother of Prince Ralis. Her death is tied to the Zora royal tragedy and she rewards Link with the Zora Armor.

### Prince Ralis
Zora prince and Queen Rutela's son. Found collapsed in Telma's care after the Lanayru events. His grief, royal identity, and relationship with his mother matter to the campaign. Ralis later helps Link identify the Reekfish/Yeto trail and provides the Coral Earring.

### Zora Royal Family / Zora community
Track their attitude toward Link, Ralis's status, and the consequences of the Twilight's effect on Zora's Domain.

### Light Spirit Lanayru
Ancient guardian of Lanayru Province. Guides Link through the restoration of Lanayru's light and contributes to the revelation of the larger Twilight crisis.

## Sacred Grove / ancient history

### Hero's Shade
Spectral warrior who teaches Link Hidden Skills. His identity and relationship to the ancient Hero should be tracked separately from ordinary NPCs. His optional appearances can become meaningful character/lore development.

### Golden Wolf
Form used in the Hero's Shade/Hidden Skill encounters. Treat the encounters as canon training opportunities when Link fulfills the conditions.

## Gerudo Desert / Arbiter's Grounds

### Sages
Ancient sages tied to the Arbiter's Grounds and the execution/banishment history of Ganondorf. They reveal important historical context and the broken Mirror of Twilight.

### Ganondorf
The true antagonist behind the larger conspiracy. His actions, knowledge, and history must remain hidden from Link until appropriately revealed.

### Bulblin / King Bulblin
Major recurring enemy force. King Bulblin is responsible for the Ordon children's abduction and later encounters Link repeatedly. His survival and later conduct should be tracked.

## Snowpeak

### Yeto
Yeti living in Snowpeak Ruins' area. Possesses a Mirror Shard and leads Link into the ruins after Link follows the Reekfish scent.

### Yeta
Yeto's wife. Her illness, the Mirror Shard, and the Snowpeak Ruins storyline form an important relationship-focused side arc. Her behavior changes during the dungeon progression.

## Hidden Village / Sky progression

### Impaz
Last resident/keeper of the Hidden Village. She possesses the key to restoring Ilia's memory and later gives Link the Ancient Sky Book after recognizing him as the legendary Heavenly Messenger.

### Dr. Borville
Physician connected to the Ilia memory restoration sequence. His role should be preserved as part of the chain leading to the Wooden Statue and memory recovery.

### Hidden Village residents
The village is largely overrun/emptied when Link arrives; its surviving residents and historical connection to the Royal Family/Heavenly Messenger lore should be preserved.

## Sky / Oocca

### Oocca
Ancient sky-dwelling people associated with the City in the Sky and the ancient technology that connects Hyrule to the heavens.

### Shad / Fyer connection
Shad's research, the Ancient Sky Book, Owl Statues, restored Dominion Rod, and Fyer's Sky Cannon repair form one continuous causal chain. Do not skip these links in the campaign.

## Twilight / endgame

### Midna
Twili companion and eventual central protagonist alongside Link. Her secretive motives, relationship with Zant, identity, and connection to the Twilight Realm must unfold over time.

### Zant
Usurper King of the Twili and servant/agent of Ganondorf. His relationship to Midna and his dependence on Ganondorf are essential to the later plot.

### Zelda
Princess of Hyrule. Her sacrifice to save Midna is a critical emotional event and affects the endgame.

### Ganondorf
Gerudo king and final antagonist. His Child Timeline history, execution by the sages, survival, relationship to the Triforce, manipulation of Zant, and final confrontation must be tracked as distinct revelations rather than dumped as exposition.

## Character importance tiers

### Tier 1 — campaign-critical recurring characters
Link, Ilia, Midna, Zelda, Ganondorf, Zant, Rusl, Colin, Telma, Prince Ralis, Renado.

### Tier 2 — major canon characters
Fado, Mayor Bo, Uli, Talo, Malo, Beth, Sera, Ooccoo, Faron, Eldin, Lanayru, Barnes, Darbus/Fyrus, Queen Rutela, Auru, Ashei, Shad, Yeto, Yeta, Impaz, Hero's Shade, King Bulblin, Oocca.

### Tier 3 — minor/optional recurring characters
Coro, Jaggle, Hanch, Pergie, Luda, Louise, Jovani, Agitha, Hena, Fyer, Plumm, Dr. Borville, Skull Kid, Great Fairies, assorted Gorons/Zoras/Ordon villagers, and other named side characters.

## Anti-forgetting rule
A character does not disappear from the campaign simply because the main quest has moved to another region. Track location, schedule, current knowledge, relationship to Link, unresolved business, and whether the canon story provides a reason they should be present or absent.
